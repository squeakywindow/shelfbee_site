Turn it on in Safari

ShelfBee lives in your Safari toolbar. Two taps and you're set.

1

Enable the extension

Open Settings → Safari → Extensions and switch on ShelfBee.

2

Add your libraries

Click the 🐝 toolbar icon and search for every library you belong to (restrictions apply).

3

Browse as usual

Open any book on a supported website — availability appears at the top.
